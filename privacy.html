Privacy Policy

Eric Kramar Studio LLC ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you submit through our contact or project inquiry forms — name, email, company name, and project details.
  • Basic technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and discuss potential projects.
  • To communicate with you about a project you've engaged us for.
  • We do not sell your information to third parties.

Third-party services

Project inquiry submissions are processed through a third-party form-delivery service to route messages to our inbox. Payment processing for e-commerce projects is handled by third-party providers (such as Stripe or PayPal) — we do not store payment card details.

Data retention

We retain inquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you or deliver a project, or as required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@erickramarstudio.com.
